# 5 Proven Study Techniques That Guarantee JAMB Success

As thousands of students prepare for JAMB 2024, the question isn't just what to study, but HOW to study effectively. At GroupXam, we've analyzed the study patterns of top-performing students and identified 5 game-changing techniques.

## 1. The 80/20 Rule for JAMB Preparation
Focus 80% of your time on the 20% of topics that appear most frequently in JAMB. Our analysis shows:
- Mathematics: Algebra and Statistics (40% of questions)
- English: Comprehension and Grammar (45% of questions)
- Biology: Genetics and Ecology (35% of questions)

## 2. Active Recall vs. Passive Reading
Replace highlighting and re-reading with active recall:
- Test yourself without looking at notes
- Explain concepts out loud
- Use our flashcard system for quick reviews

## 3. Spaced Repetition Schedule
Review material using this proven timeline:
- Day 1: Learn new concept
- Day 3: Quick review (5 minutes)
- Day 7: Practice test
- Day 21: Final review

## 4. Mock Exam Strategy
Take weekly mock exams with these rules:
- Simulate real exam conditions
- Time yourself strictly
- Analyze wrong answers immediately
- Focus next week's study on weak areas

## 5. Subject Rotation Technique
Rotate subjects every 45 minutes to prevent mental fatigue:
- 45 min Mathematics
- 15 min break
- 45 min English
- 15 min break
- 45 min Science subject

Start practicing with GroupXam's comprehensive JAMB preparation tools. Our platform makes it easy to apply these proven techniques with:
- Timed practice tests

- Progress tracking
- Weak area identification
